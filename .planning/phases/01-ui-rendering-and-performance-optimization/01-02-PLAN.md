<objective>
Optimize scrolling performance and list item recycling across HomeScreen, SearchScreen, and LibraryScreen. Enforce explicit contentType definitions, stable keys, and ensure Haze blur boundaries do not cause GPU bottlenecks during rapid flings.
</objective>

<execution_context>
- Codebase map: `.planning/codebase/ARCHITECTURE.md`, `CONVENTIONS.md`, `STRUCTURE.md`
- Context: `.planning/phases/01-ui-rendering-and-performance-optimization/01-CONTEXT.md`

</execution_context>

<context>
During rapid scrolling across large music catalogs and carousels, missing `contentType` hints force Compose to recreate nodes instead of recycling existing Composable layouts. Furthermore, unconstrained blur effects behind scrolling feeds can saturate GPU fill-rate.
</context>

    - sharedUi/src/jvmSharedMain/kotlin/com/music/bitchord/ui/screens/LibraryScreen.kt

  <action>
